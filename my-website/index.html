With over 11 years of experience in the field of psychiatry, Dr. Amit Sonawane is known for his calm, empathetic, and non-judgmental approach towards patients facing a wide range of mental health challenges.

He has extensive clinical experience in the diagnosis and treatment of conditions such as depression, anxiety disorders, OCD, schizophrenia, bipolar disorders, sexual disorders, sleep disorders, eating disorders, child and adolescent mental health problems.

He has a special interest in addiction psychiatry and has received specialized formal training in the treatment of various addictions, including alcohol, cannabis, tobacco, drugs, gambling, and mobile/internet addiction.

He is committed to providing evidence-based, compassionate, and individualized care tailored to each patient’s unique needs.
